How We Work

RFP to Delivery

01

Procurement Review

Send us the RFP number and link. We will review it within 48 hours and tell you if we are bidding. If we are not the right vendor, we will say so rather than waste procurement time.

02

30-Minute Discovery Call

If the RFP is a fit, we schedule a call to clarify requirements and confirm our understanding. This is optional but useful for avoiding assumptions that surface later as change orders.

03

Response & Submission

We write the technical sections, including architecture diagrams, delivery milestones, and team profiles. One point of contact coordinates the full response.

04

Fixed-Scope Delivery

If awarded, we deliver to the agreed scope and timeline. Working software in front of you on a regular cadence, so direction changes stay cheap and issues surface early.
