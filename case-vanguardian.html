VanGuardian - Fall Detection Without Wearables

An AI-powered fall detection system that protects seniors and vulnerable individuals without requiring them to wear any device - safety in every stumble.

Overview

Falls are the leading cause of injury among seniors, and traditional fall detection relies on wearable devices that are often forgotten, uncomfortable, or not charged. VanGuardian is our own product - built from the ground up by The Alpha Nova - that uses AI and sensor technology to detect falls in real-time without the person needing to wear anything at all.

The Challenge

  • Traditional wearable-based fall detection has low adoption rates among seniors
  • Detecting falls accurately without body-worn sensors while minimizing false alarms
  • Ensuring real-time alerting to caregivers and emergency contacts within seconds
  • Maintaining privacy - no cameras or video recording in personal living spaces
  • Building a system that works reliably 24/7 with minimal maintenance

Our Solution

  • AI-Powered Detection: Machine learning models trained to distinguish falls from normal daily activities with high accuracy
  • No Wearables Required: Ambient sensor technology that monitors the environment, not the person - nothing to charge, wear, or remember
  • Instant Alerts: Automated notifications sent to caregivers, family members, and emergency services within seconds of a detected fall
  • Privacy-First Design: No cameras or video - only non-invasive sensors that respect the dignity and privacy of users
  • Caregiver Dashboard: Real-time monitoring interface for care facilities and family members to track alerts and activity patterns
