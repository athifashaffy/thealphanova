This Privacy Policy explains how the Animal Facts mobile application ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed with The Alpha Nova Inc., handles information when you use it. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

01

Information We Collect

Animal Facts is designed to respect your privacy. You can use the App without creating an account, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. The App also does not display advertising.

The only information that may be processed is limited, anonymous, and non-identifying:

  • Basic diagnostic and usage data: standard app-platform services (such as Google Play) may collect anonymous, aggregated information like app version, device type, operating system version, and crash reports. This helps keep the App stable and is not linked to your identity.

02

How We Use Information

  • To keep the App working reliably and to fix crashes or technical problems.
  • To understand general, anonymous usage so we can improve the App.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

03

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information, because we do not collect it. Any anonymous diagnostic data described above is handled by the platform provider (Google Play) under its own Privacy Policy. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

04

Children's Privacy

The App is family-friendly and may appeal to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the App contains no advertising. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly. Our practices are intended to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

05

Data Retention and Security

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data for us to retain. Any anonymous diagnostic data is managed by the platform provider. We use reasonable measures to keep the App secure, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

06

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights regarding personal data. Since the App does not collect personal data, there is typically nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions or requests, contact us using the details below.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
