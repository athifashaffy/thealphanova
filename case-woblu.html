Woblu

An app that connects homeowners with trusted local handypeople, from plumbing emergencies to carpentry projects and everything in between. What started as an award-winning student idea grew into a real home-services marketplace.

Overview

Woblu is an on-demand app that connects people with trusted handypeople for everyday household needs. From plumbing emergencies to carpentry projects, Woblu helps homeowners find the right professional to get the job done, and helps skilled workers find local jobs near them.

The idea began as "Work4U," a student innovation project co-created by The Alpha Nova founder Athif Shaffy. It won 1st place and the Best Use of Technology prize at the Cambrian R&D 2023 Student Innovation Challenge in Sudbury. Work4U later became Woblu, and the venture secured $18,000 in funding through the Costarter startup accelerator.

The Idea

  • Give homeowners a fast, reliable way to find trusted plumbers, carpenters, electricians, and gardeners for small jobs
  • Let people post a job with photos and a description, then get matched with available professionals nearby
  • Help skilled workers discover local work and grow their business
  • Replace the hassle of endlessly searching for dependable service providers

What We Built

  • On-Demand Marketplace: A two-sided mobile app matching customers with local skilled workers in real time
  • Job Posting Flow: Customers post requirements with photos and descriptions; professionals accept and get started
  • Trust & Reliability: A focus on connecting users with vetted, dependable handypeople
  • Mobile-First Experience: A clean, intuitive interface designed to make booking help effortless
  • From Pitch to Product: Taken from an award-winning concept through to a working app and accelerator venture
