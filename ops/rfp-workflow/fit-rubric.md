# Fit rubric (GO / MAYBE / NO-GO)

Score every opportunity before ChatGPT or Claude writes a word.

## Automatic NO-GO

- Construction, civil, snow, paving, fleet vehicles, medical devices we do not make
- Requires a live Canadian municipal 311 / SAP PM / Esri production reference we do not have, and scoring makes that mandatory
- US-only supplier restriction we cannot meet, or a ban on Canadian remote delivery we cannot meet
- Close is inside 48 hours and no draft exists
- We would have to invent experience to look competitive

## Strong GO (write a draft)

Canadian buyer. Software, data portal, website platform, AI/automation, IoT, assessment/advisory, or product engineering.

AND at least two of:

- Honest relevant work (COGITO, Wheel It, Cambrian, mining/vision, custom web)
- Budget / term we can staff
- Portal we already have (MERX or a bidsandtenders vendor account)
- Close is at least five calendar days out, or a draft already exists

## MAYBE

- Adjacent (CCTV software, GIS-heavy, large ERP integration) where we can be honest about the gap
- Defence / dual-use: route through AN Labs positioning, do not make the main company look defence-only
