## Questions

### 1. CRM solution form

Does CCMEC expect the CRM component to be a custom-built solution, a configured commercial off-the-shelf platform, or will either approach be accepted if the proponent meets the functional, security, portability, and integration requirements?

**Why it matters for the bid:** The answer determines the architecture, implementation effort, licence model, assurance evidence, and first-year cost.

### 2. Tier 2 identity provider

What identity provider, authentication protocol, and user population must the solution use for authenticated Tier 2 access?

**Why it matters for the bid:** The answer is required to design role-based access correctly and to price the authentication integration without assuming an identity platform.

### 3. SOC 2 or ISO evidence

For the SOC 2 or ISO 27001 requirement, must the proponent company hold the assurance report or certification directly, or may the requirement be met through the proposed hosted platform and relevant subprocessors if the proponent supplies the applicable reports, control mapping, DPA, and subprocessor disclosures?

**Why it matters for the bid:** The answer determines bidder eligibility, the evidence package, and which hosted architecture can be proposed accurately.

### 4. CRM seats and user roles

How many named and concurrent CRM users should proponents price, and how many of those users require administrator, knowledge-editor, supervisor, or agent permissions?

**Why it matters for the bid:** CRM licence costs and access-control design depend on the number and type of seats, not only the stated three-person CICIC team.

### 5. YoVu integration path

For the telephone callbot, should proponents integrate with the current YoVu service through SIP, an available API, or the existing IVR and number-routing configuration, or may they propose replacing the current IVR while retaining CICIC's telephone number and transfer paths?

**Why it matters for the bid:** The supported YoVu interface determines the call architecture, transfer and callback design, implementation risk, carrier dependency, and usage pricing.

### 6. Mailbot and email workflow boundary

Within the CRM scope and any referenced mailbot or channel A capability, which functions are required in the current procurement: inbound email ingestion and tagging, AI-assisted drafts held for staff approval, outbound delivery after staff approval, or only future extensibility?

**Why it matters for the bid:** The answer prevents optional email automation from being omitted or priced as a required fourth channel and defines the human-review control before external send.

### 7. Canadian residency under R7

Would deployment in a Canadian public-cloud region such as `ca-central-1` satisfy the R7 residency preference, or does CICIC prefer or require a private or on-premises deployment, and must the same location rule cover model inference, logs, backups, and telephony media?

**Why it matters for the bid:** The answer determines the deployable architecture, subprocessor chain, evidence, operating cost, and whether any cross-border service is acceptable.

### 8. Official question-submission channel

The notice indicates that questions are not submitted online; should proponents email questions directly to Michael Ringuette at m.ringuette@cmec.ca, and are there required subject-line, copy, attachment, or MERX reference instructions?

**Why it matters for the bid:** The Alpha Nova needs to use the valid channel before the deadline and avoid having a timely clarification treated as not received.

### 9. EV-01 evaluation material

For EV-01, will CICIC provide all English and French sample inquiries and the authoritative CICIC knowledge items against which answers will be evaluated, and at what stage will that material be made available to proponents?

**Why it matters for the bid:** Closed-domain performance should be demonstrated on CICIC-approved material rather than on proponent-created questions or content inferred from the public web.

### 10. Page, format, and appendix limits

Please confirm the page limit, font and margin rules, permitted file formats and sizes, and whether resumes, the R1-R22 compliance matrix, security evidence, accessibility evidence, forms, and pricing schedules count toward the technical-response limit.

**Why it matters for the bid:** The answer determines how the response is packaged and ensures mandatory evidence is included without breaching an unstated format rule.
