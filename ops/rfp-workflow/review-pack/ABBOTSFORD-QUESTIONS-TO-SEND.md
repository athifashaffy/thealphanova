## Questions to post

**Q1. SAP landscape and interface.** Addendum 1 identifies SAP Plant Maintenance transactions IW21, IW26 and IW31. Can the City confirm (a) the SAP release and whether it is on-premises or cloud, (b) whether an integration interface already exists for external systems (for example OData services on SAP Gateway, RFC-enabled BAPIs through middleware, or SAP BTP / PI/PO integration flows), and (c) whether exposing or extending that interface is the City's or its SAP partner's responsibility rather than the proponent's?

**Q2. CentralSquare Works API and sandbox.** Can the City confirm that the CentralSquare Works API is available to a third-party integrator under the City's licence, that a sandbox or test tenant will be provided, and the planned cut-over date from v25.3 to the v26.1 cloud release, so the connector is built against the target version?

**Q3. Esri endpoints and network path.** Can the City provide, at or before contract execution, the ArcGIS Enterprise 11.5 and ArcGIS Online service URLs in scope, the authentication model for a third-party application, and confirmation of whether a cloud-hosted integration layer may reach the on-premises Enterprise instance directly or through a City-hosted relay or reverse proxy?

**Q4. Canadian personal information residency.** Does the City require that all resident personal information collected by the platform be stored and processed in Canada, and are there any further residency, access or sub-processor conditions under FIPPA or City policy the proponent must meet?

**Q5. Incumbent export and migration.** Can the City obtain a full export of resident accounts, open service requests, history and media from the current mobile platform, in what format, and is the proponent expected to migrate that history into the new platform, into CentralSquare Works, or both?

**Q6. WorkSafeBC and business licence for an out-of-province remote vendor.** For a proponent incorporated and operating in Ontario with remote delivery and no employees working in British Columbia, does the City require WorkSafeBC registration or clearance and a City of Abbotsford business licence at award, and if so at what point?

**Q7. App store ownership and identity provider.** Will the applications be published under the City's own Apple Developer and Google Play developer accounts, and will staff single sign-on be provided through the City's identity provider (for example Microsoft Entra ID)? Please confirm the identity provider in use.

**Q8. Go-live versus contract execution.** The RFP indicates contract execution on or about 30 October 2026. Does the City have a required or target go-live date for the resident application and for the staff functionality, and are there blackout periods to avoid?
