# Do this on your PC

This cloud box cannot log into your ChatGPT or Claude. Run the writing steps in the browser tabs already open on your computer.

Double-click `open-on-pc.html`, or run `run-local.ps1` (Windows) / `run-local.sh` (Mac). That opens ChatGPT, Claude, MERX, and the Drive folder.

## Every bid (10 minutes of pasting)

1. On your PC, open ChatGPT (you are already logged in).
2. Paste the ChatGPT Project instructions from `prompts/chatgpt-writer.md` once. Pin them as a Project if you can.
3. Second message: RFP title, close date, and the official PDF (or a clean extract).
4. Copy ChatGPT's draft.
5. On your PC, open Claude (you are already logged in).
6. Paste `prompts/claude-compliance.md`, attach the official PDF, paste ChatGPT's draft.
7. Copy Claude's cleaned draft into a Google Doc in this Drive folder, still marked INTERNAL DRAFT:
   https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1OlzaMxK54cpJyfcwu8r86wBAUKfZGm5V
8. You submit on MERX or the municipal portal. Nothing submits without your yes. Never on close day.
