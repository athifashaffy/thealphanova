Cogito AI

Evidence-based mental-health therapy, delivered as software people keep coming back to. Built with Prof. Steffen Moritz at University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf and validated in peer-reviewed research.

The Problem

Good Therapy That Nobody Finishes Helps No One

Traditional mental-health interventions run into the same three walls: low engagement, limited accessibility, and adherence that falls away over the length of a programme. The clinical content was never the bottleneck — the delivery was.

Capabilities

What Cogito AI Does

Gamified MCT Exercises

Points, achievements and progression that turn therapeutic modules into something patients actually complete — without loosening clinical validity.

Clinician Dashboard

Real-time analytics so therapists can see patient progress and adjust treatment plans, rather than waiting for the next appointment to find out.

Adaptive Difficulty

AI-driven adjustment based on patient performance and engagement, so the programme meets people where they are.

Multi-Language

Full localization for German, English and further languages, built for deployment across different healthcare systems.

Offline Capable

Complete functionality with no connection required. Accessibility matters most for the people least likely to have reliable internet.

GDPR & Encryption

End-to-end encryption and strict, compliant data handling designed for European clinical deployment.

Cogito AI is our platform for mental-health technology, built on the COGITO app developed with Prof. Steffen Moritz at University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf (UKE). It delivers metacognitive therapy (MCT) exercises as gamified, interactive modules, with a clinician dashboard behind them.

The underlying COGITO platform has been validated through peer-reviewed research published in scientific journals, demonstrating effectiveness in improving mental health outcomes. It was built in partnership with a university hospital rather than adapted for one afterwards.
