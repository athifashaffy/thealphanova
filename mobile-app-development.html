Capabilities

What We Build

Apps that survive the app store review, the security questionnaire and the second year of maintenance.

Cross-Platform Apps

One codebase serving iOS and Android at native quality, which is usually the right economics unless you have a specific reason to go native.

Native iOS & Android

Fully native builds where the app depends on deep platform integration, demanding performance or specialised hardware access.

Offline-First Design

Local persistence and sync so the app keeps working underground, in transit or anywhere the signal drops — then reconciles cleanly when it returns.

Notifications & Engagement

Scheduled and event-driven notifications that respect the user rather than nagging them, with the analytics to see what actually drives return visits.

Regulated & Clinical Apps

Applications handling health and research data, built with the consent flows, audit trails and data handling those settings require.

Store Launch & Iteration

Release pipelines, store submission, staged rollout and the ongoing update cadence that keeps an app from quietly rotting.
