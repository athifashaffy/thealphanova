Common questions about our software development services in Sudbury and Northern Ontario

What software development services does The Alpha Nova offer in Sudbury?

The Alpha Nova offers comprehensive software development services in Sudbury including custom mobile app development, web application development, AI and machine learning solutions, database engineering, UI/UX design, and enterprise software development. We serve clients across Northern Ontario and globally.

Where is The Alpha Nova located in Sudbury?

The Alpha Nova is headquartered at 1545 Maley Drive in Greater Sudbury, Ontario. Our Sudbury office serves as the hub for our North American operations, with additional offices in Passau, Germany and Colombo, Sri Lanka.

How much does custom software development cost in Sudbury?

Custom software development costs vary based on project complexity, features, and timeline. At The Alpha Nova, we provide transparent pricing and work with businesses of all sizes across Northern Ontario. Contact us at info@thealphanova.com or +1-437-424-5384 for a detailed quote tailored to your specific needs.

What industries does The Alpha Nova serve in Northern Ontario?

We serve diverse industries including mining (Côté Gold AI competition winner), healthcare (COGITO app with UKE Hamburg), education (Cambrian College Alumni App), transportation, aviation, and enterprise businesses. Our Sudbury location gives us unique insight into Northern Ontario's mining and resource sectors.

How long does it take to develop custom software?

Development timelines depend on project scope. Simple mobile apps can take 2-3 months, while complex enterprise systems may take 6-12 months. At The Alpha Nova, we follow agile development methodologies with regular milestones and transparent communication throughout the project.

Does The Alpha Nova work with startups and small businesses?

Yes! We work with businesses of all sizes, from startups to enterprises. Many of our successful projects started as startup ideas, including our own VanGuardian fall detection system. We're passionate about helping Northern Ontario businesses and startups turn their ideas into reality.
