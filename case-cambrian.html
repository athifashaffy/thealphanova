Cambrian College Alumni App

A comprehensive mobile application connecting Cambrian College alumni with networking opportunities, events, career resources, and a vibrant community platform.

Overview

Cambrian College needed a modern mobile platform to engage their growing alumni network. The existing communication channels were fragmented and ineffective at fostering meaningful connections between graduates, the institution, and potential employers.

The Challenge

  • Low alumni engagement with existing communication channels
  • No centralized platform for networking and career opportunities
  • Difficulty promoting and managing alumni events at scale
  • Fragmented alumni data across multiple legacy systems
  • Need for both iOS and Android support with a unified experience

Our Solution

  • Cross-Platform App: Native-quality mobile app for iOS and Android with shared codebase
  • Alumni Directory: Searchable network with filters for industry, graduation year, and location
  • Event Management: Full event lifecycle from creation to RSVP tracking and post-event engagement
  • Career Portal: Job postings, mentorship matching, and career resource library
  • Push Notifications: Targeted communications based on alumni interests and engagement history
  • Content Hub: News, success stories, and institutional updates in a personalized feed

Impact

User Satisfaction
  • Tripled alumni engagement within the first 6 months of launch
  • Successfully facilitated hundreds of professional connections
  • Streamlined event management reducing administrative overhead by 60%
  • Became the primary communication channel for alumni relations
