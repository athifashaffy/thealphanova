COGITO Self-Help App

Engineering partner on the free COGITO self-help app with University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf (UKE). We built the Flutter platform that makes Prof. Steffen Moritz's Metacognitive Training exercises accessible as a usable daily practice tool.

Overview

COGITO is a free self-help app that makes short CBT and Metacognitive Training (MCT) exercises accessible for everyday practice. Developed by Prof. Steffen Moritz's research group at UKE Hamburg, the library has been studied in multiple published RCTs. The Alpha Nova and PatientZero have been the engineering partners since 2021, building the product that delivers those exercises.

The app collects no personal data, requires no account, and runs with no ads. UKE owns the clinical exercises and the research line. We built the platform.

The Challenge

Prof. Moritz's research group had validated MCT exercises, but they existed as PDFs and paper workbooks. To work as daily practice, the exercises needed to be mobile, multilingual, usable offline, and able to keep the privacy promise (no personal data collection). The technical requirement was straightforward: make this library accessible without compromising on privacy.

Our Role

We built the Flutter app and the infrastructure that makes the COGITO exercise library usable as a daily practice tool:

  • Cross-Platform Flutter App: One codebase delivering the full exercise library on iOS and Android, with offline capability so exercises work without connectivity.
  • Privacy-First Architecture: No account creation, no personal data collection, no analytics that identify users. The promise is not optional marketing copy, it is how the system is built.
  • Multi-Language Support: Full localization for German, English, and additional languages, so the exercises reach a broader community.
  • Daily Practice Features: Progress tracking (stored locally), reminders, and a structured library so users can return to exercises consistently.
  • Cogito AI Companion: A separate on-device AI layer we built to help users navigate the exercise library. The companion reads only the lines the team wrote. It is not a therapist, not diagnosis, and not therapy.

What we did not build: a clinician dashboard, real-time patient analytics, an algorithm that adjusts difficulty, or therapy. COGITO is a self-help tool. The four published RCTs are on the exercise app, not the AI companion layer.
