COGITO Gamification App

A groundbreaking gamified mental health app developed in collaboration with the University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf (UKE), delivering evidence-based therapeutic interventions through engaging game mechanics.

Overview

COGITO is a mobile mental health app that uses gamification principles to deliver metacognitive therapy (MCT) exercises. Developed in partnership with Prof. Steffen Moritz at UKE Hamburg, the app makes evidence-based psychological interventions accessible and engaging for patients.

The platform has been validated through peer-reviewed research published in scientific journals, demonstrating its effectiveness in improving mental health outcomes.

The Challenge

Traditional mental health interventions face significant barriers: low patient engagement, limited accessibility, and difficulty maintaining long-term adherence to therapeutic programs. UKE Hamburg needed a digital solution that could:

  • Make complex metacognitive therapy exercises intuitive and engaging
  • Support multiple languages for wider deployment
  • Track patient progress and provide actionable insights to clinicians
  • Maintain clinical validity while delivering an enjoyable user experience
  • Scale to support thousands of concurrent users across different healthcare systems

Our Solution

We designed and built a comprehensive gamified app that transforms therapeutic exercises into interactive game-like experiences, featuring:

  • Gamified MCT Exercises: Points, achievements, and progression systems that motivate patients to complete therapeutic modules
  • Multi-Language Support: Full localization for German, English, and additional languages for broader reach
  • Clinician Dashboard: Real-time analytics allowing therapists to monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans
  • Adaptive Difficulty: AI-driven difficulty adjustment based on patient performance and engagement patterns
  • Offline Capability: Full functionality even without internet connection for maximum accessibility
  • GDPR Compliance: End-to-end encryption and strict data privacy controls meeting European healthcare regulations
